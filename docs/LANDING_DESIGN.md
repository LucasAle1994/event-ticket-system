# Landing Design Specification

## Purpose

The landing page is the first contact between the visitor and the event.

Its only objective is to convert visitors into registered participants.

Every design decision should help the visitor feel identified, build trust and complete the registration form.

The landing must never feel like a software product or a corporate website.

It should feel warm, professional and human.

---

# Communication Tone

The copy should sound like it was written by a real person.

Avoid marketing clichés.

Avoid exaggerated promises.

Avoid AI-like expressions.

Never use phrases like:

- Discover your potential
- Transform your life
- Unlock your future
- Join an unforgettable experience

Instead:

Speak naturally.

Speak with empathy.

Speak to parents.

Every paragraph should make the visitor think:

"This is exactly what I'm going through."

---

# Visual Identity

The visual identity must be inspired by the official flyer.

Do NOT copy the flyer literally.

Adapt it into a premium website.

The landing should feel calm, elegant and trustworthy.

# Registration

The registration form must NOT be permanently visible on the landing page.

Registration will be handled through a floating modal.

The primary CTAs:

- Reservar mi lugar
- Inscribirme

must open the registration modal.

The modal itself will be implemented during Sprint 003.

Sprint 002B should only ensure that the CTA interaction is prepared without implementing the registration business logic or form.

The landing page must remain focused on the event and should not contain a large registration section at the bottom.
